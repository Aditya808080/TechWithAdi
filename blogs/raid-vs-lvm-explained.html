RAID vs LVM Explained (Hindi Guide)

Linux administrators ko RAID aur LVM dono concepts samajhna bahut zaruri hai. Dono storage management se related hain, lekin dono ka purpose alag hota hai.

RAID Kya Hai?

RAID (Redundant Array of Independent Disks) multiple disks ko combine karke performance aur fault tolerance provide karta hai.

RAID ka main goal: Data Protection aur Performance.

Popular RAID Levels

RAID 0

Data striping karta hai. Performance fast hoti hai lekin disk fail hone par data loss ho sakta hai.

RAID 1

Mirroring use karta hai. Ek disk fail ho jaye to data safe rehta hai.

RAID 5

Parity ke saath fault tolerance provide karta hai.

RAID 10

RAID 1 aur RAID 0 ka combination. Performance aur redundancy dono milte hain.

LVM Kya Hai?

LVM (Logical Volume Manager) Linux me flexible storage management provide karta hai.

LVM ka main goal: Storage ko easily resize aur manage karna.

LVM Components

  • Physical Volume (PV)
  • Volume Group (VG)
  • Logical Volume (LV)

LVM Commands

pvcreate /dev/sdb
vgcreate data_vg /dev/sdb
lvcreate -L 10G -n data_lv data_vg

RAID vs LVM Comparison

Feature RAID LVM
Performance Yes No
Redundancy Yes No
Resize Storage Limited Excellent
Snapshots No Yes

Real World Example

Production server me aksar RAID + LVM dono saath use kiye jaate hain.

Disks -> RAID 1
RAID Device -> LVM
LVM -> Logical Volumes

Is approach se data protection aur flexible storage management dono milte hain.

FAQ

Kya LVM RAID ka replacement hai?
Nahi, dono alag purpose serve karte hain.

Kya RAID aur LVM ek saath use kar sakte hain?
Haan, production servers me commonly use kiya jata hai.

Conclusion

RAID performance aur redundancy ke liye use hota hai, jabki LVM storage flexibility ke liye. Best practice ye hai ki critical servers me RAID aur LVM dono ka combination use kiya jaye.
